# Code of Conduct

We want Tako to be a project where people can work together without fear of
harassment. This is a short version in the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) 2.1.

## Our standard

Do: be respectful, assume good faith, give and accept constructive feedback,
focus on what is best for the project and its users.

Do not: harass, insult, demean or discriminate; publish others' private
information; make sexualized comments or advances; or otherwise behave in ways
that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and ban people
who break these rules. Report problems privately to the maintainers through
the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (a dedicated conduct address will be
added when the repository has a home). Reports are handled confidentially and
without retaliation.

The full Contributor Covenant 2.1 text applies where this summary is silent.
